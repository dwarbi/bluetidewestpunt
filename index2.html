🏠 About This Property

Welcome to Blue Tide Westpunt, a private top-floor retreat in the gated Punta Azul community at the far western tip of Curaçao. This beautifully appointed vacation rental puts you moments from the sea — surrounded by coral reefs and Caribbean sunshine.

Our unit features two spacious bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a complete kitchen, in-unit washer/dryer, and air conditioning throughout. The community offers a gorgeous pool and direct water access with no streets to cross.

Owners Denis & Joanna offer direct booking — no platform fees, personal communication, and the best rates available anywhere online.
