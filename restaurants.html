Westpunt to Tera Kora

Where to Eat in Banda Abou

Restaurants from Westpunt down to Tera Kora. Not every place out here, just the ones worth the stop, with what to order and when they're actually open.

Use WhatsApp. It's how nearly every business on the island communicates. Message a restaurant before you drive to check it's open or to reserve.

Hours last checked October 2026. They change often out here.

Westpunt

The fishing village at the western tip, around Playa Forti, Playa Piskadó and Playa Kalki.

Restaurant Playa Forti

Daily 10:30 AM–7 PM (to 8 PM Sat–Sun)

Clifftop restaurant above Playa Forti with a wide-open view of the bay. Fresh fish and local dishes, plus vegetarian options. The cliff jumpers launch from right beside the terrace.

Tip Ask for a table along the cliff edge, and come for sunset.

Directions +599 9 868 8294

Blue View Sunset Terrace

Daily 11 AM–8 PM

Also above Playa Forti, with tables on a glass floor about 15–20 meters over the beach and rocks. Local cooking: ribs and stobas (beef, goat, papaya) with funchi. Look for the blue Fiat 500 at the entrance.

Tip Come for the view and a drink at sunset. Service can be slow, so don't come in a hurry.

Directions +599 9 864 6013

Jaanchie's Restaurant

Lunch only

1–5 PM, closed Friday

A family restaurant going back generations, serving traditional Curaçaoan food. There's no printed menu; Jaanchie's son Johan comes to the table and talks you through the day's dishes. Goat stew (kabritu stoba), fresh fish, and iguana if you're curious. Open-air, with feeders that keep the place full of small birds.

Tip It's a lunch place now. Go for the experience as much as the food.

Directions +599 9 864 0126

Sol Food

Weekends only

Fri–Sun, 12–8 PM

Weekend pizza, grilled fish and good salads on a breezy upstairs deck at Sunshine Getaway. Run by Sunshine and David, and you'll probably chat with them. Only about a dozen tables.

Tip Reserve. It fills up, especially for dinner.

Directions +599 9 864 0005

Sabor di Playa

Sundays only

Sundays only, 9 AM–4 PM

Local Sunday food a few steps from Playa Piskadó: the catch of the day, fried, with sides like tutu (cornmeal with beans). The menu is in Papiamentu and the staff are happy to explain it.

Tip The mahi (dradu) is big enough for two. Get a cold coconut with it.

Directions +599 9 699 6739

Isabelle off the Beach

Thu 5–9 PM · Fri–Sat 12–9 PM · Sun 12–8 PM · closed Mon–Wed

A garden restaurant in the old Landhuis Misjé, a little more upscale. Steak, red snapper, tacos and blackened tuna, with rum cake for dessert. Vegetarian and gluten-free options. Live music some nights.

Tip Reserve, especially on weekends.

Directions +599 9 667 5362

Cactus Café

Mon–Thu 5–9 PM · Sat–Sun 12–3 PM and 5–9 PM · closed Friday

Recently moved to a new spot close to Playa Kalki. Known for the African peanut soup with banana, the stobas, and catch of the day in coconut or lemon-caper sauce.

Tip Book ahead, even when it looks quiet.

Directions +599 9 513 7535

Mama Kunuku

Daily 8 AM–10 PM

The restaurant at Bed & Bike Westpunt, open to everyone. “Kunuku” means countryside. Many of the vegetables come from their own garden, and the meats are slow-roasted over hardwood charcoal. Breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Tip One of the few places in Westpunt serving breakfast, and open every day.

Directions +599 9 888 0354 Website

Kòfi Kalki

Coffee

Daily 7:45 AM–3:30 PM

A small coffee bar at water level at Playa Kalki. Excellent espresso with the sea right in front of you. No meals, but there's pie and homemade ice cream.

Tip Park in the lot and take the stairs down toward the beach.

Directions

Christoffel

At the entrance to Christoffel National Park, on the northern road.

Kofihowz

Coffee & light food

7 AM–2 PM, closed Tuesday

Right outside the entrance to Christoffel Park. Coffee, homemade cakes, fresh juices and light bites like egg buns. The perfect place to relax while others scramble to the top. Say hello to Tariq and Howard.

Tip Try the moringa cake. Good before the climb or after.

Directions +599 9 526 3839

Lagun

A small village around one of the best snorkeling coves on the island.

Bahia Restaurant

Daily 9 AM–9 PM

On the cliff above Playa Lagun, looking straight down into the cove. Seafood is the thing here: order the catch of the day. Breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Tip Good for lunch between snorkels, or at sunset. Service runs on island time.

Directions +599 9 864 1000

Dibario

Closed Tuesday. Hours aren't posted, so call ahead.

Home-style criollo cooking near Playa Lagun: stewed chicken, karni stoba, ribs, fish, with rice and plantains. Big portions, low prices.

Tip If nobody's around, ring the bell. The cook will come out and tell you what's on today.

Directions +599 9 864 5555

Santa Cruz

Inland from Playa Santa Cruz, around the Hòfi Mango park.

Kultura Kòrsou

At Hòfi Mango

Wed–Sun 10 AM–6 PM (kitchen closes 4 PM, bar bites until 5) · closed Mon–Tue

The restaurant inside the Hòfi Mango park. Chef Willy Balentina cooks Creole food with fresh local ingredients, with the stated aim of raising Curaçao cuisine “to star level.” Brunch until noon, including pumpkin pancakes with mango caramel.

Tip No reservations, so expect a short wait at busy times. Pair it with a walk through the mango forest.

Directions +599 9 661 0354 Website

Shelterrock Paradise

Mon 4–8 PM · Tue–Thu 1:30–8 PM · Fri 6:30–11 PM · Sat 12–8 PM · Sun 12–6 PM

Family-run, with a menu built on local dishes and island herbs, including vegetarian meals. Friday is Coconut Night: your main course is cooked for hours inside a fresh coconut over hot coals and served with a buffet of local sides, starting at 7 PM.

Tip Reserve Coconut Night well in advance. WhatsApp +599 9 682 0160.

Directions +599 9 682 0160 Website

Barber

The main village on the northern road, about halfway to Westpunt.

Marshe di Barber

Sunday market

Sundays only, 7:30 AM–7 PM

A small local market with produce, baked goods and a few food stalls. This is what people here eat on a Sunday: fish soup, johnny cakes, stews, and arepa di pampuna (pumpkin pancakes).

Tip Go in the morning, and get the pumpkin pancakes. A good stop after climbing Christoffel.

Directions

Komedor Krioyo

Landhuis Dokterstuin

Tue–Sun 10 AM–5 PM, closed Monday. Call to confirm.

Traditional krioyo cooking in an old plantation house with historical objects on display. Oxtail, goat stew, conch, whole red snapper, papaya stew, with funchi, tutu and fried plantain.

Tip Go for lunch. Popular dishes run out later in the afternoon.

Directions +599 9 864 2701

Soto & Cas Abou

The coast road between Santa Cruz and the turnoff for Cas Abou and Porto Mari.

Landhuis Klein Santa Martha

Daily, about 8 AM–10 PM. Call to confirm.

A restored plantation house on a hilltop above Soto, now a small hotel with a restaurant open to everyone. Caribbean dishes like karni stoba alongside pasta and cheese fondue, with a view down to the water.

Tip Come before sunset for the view, and call to reserve.

Directions +599 9 864 0316

Bistro e Lanternu

Dinner only

Thu–Mon 5–8:30 PM, closed Tue–Wed

A small Dutch-run bistro in a garden yard near the Cas Abou turnoff. Spare ribs, beef tenderloin, peanut soup, a monthly special, and homemade desserts from the pastry cabinet.

Tip Cash only, and reserve. Good for dinner on the way back from Cas Abou or Porto Mari.

Directions +599 9 520 7249

Sint Willibrordus

Flamingo country: the salt pans, the WILLIWOOD sign, and Coral Estate on the coast.

Marfa's Good Hangout

10 AM–10 PM (Sun from 11 AM), closed Tuesday

Restaurant, bar, souvenir shop and corner store in one, across from the WILLIWOOD sign. Home of the Williburger, a goat burger made entirely from island ingredients, plus goat stew and the daily catch.

Tip Live music some evenings. An easy lunch stop after Porto Mari or the flamingos.

Directions +599 9 512 1124

Karakter

Beach club

Daily 8 AM–9 PM

Beach, lounge and restaurant right on the sand at Coral Estate. Breakfast through dinner with your feet near the water.

Tip Reserve online for dinner at sunset. Same-day bookings are by phone only.

Directions +599 9 864 2233 Website

Koraal Rooftop Terrace

Daily 10 AM–10 PM

Karakter's sister spot up the hill at Coral Estate: a rooftop bar and restaurant with a 270-degree sea view from every seat. Drinks, bites and dinner, with occasional theme nights like sushi.

Tip Reserve a front table for sunset. Prices are on the higher side.

Directions +599 9 735 2884 Website

Tera Kora

Where Banda Abou begins, just past the windmills.

Landhuis Daniel

Breakfast and lunch 8 AM–2 PM, dinner 5–10 PM (Mon from noon)

A plantation house from around 1700 with tables on a covered garden patio and the chef-owner's paintings on the walls. Known for Dutch pancakes at breakfast and lunch; dinner ranges from French classics to an Indonesian rice table.

Tip Go for breakfast or lunch and order the pancakes.

Directions +599 9 864 8400
