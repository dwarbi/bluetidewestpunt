Good to know

Westpunt is about 45 minutes from the airport, and you'll want a rental car. You get your own parking spot right at the building. Once you're here, the closest beaches are five to ten minutes away.

Where We Are

Westpunt, at the far end of the island

Westpunt is a small fishing village at the tip of Bandabou, the quiet, green half of Curaçao. It's a long way from the cruise port and the resort strip, and close to the beaches most visitors drive an hour to reach.

Curaçao sits outside the hurricane belt, so it's warm and breezy all year.
