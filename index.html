The Setting

Remote, unspoiled, impossibly beautiful.

Nestled at the far western tip of Curaçao, Westpunt is a tranquil fishing village that embodies the unspoiled beauty of the Caribbean. This serene paradise offers a peaceful escape from busy tourist areas, with dramatic coastal landscapes, pristine beaches, and a laid-back island atmosphere that invites you to slow down and breathe deeply.

Located just outside the hurricane belt, Curaçao enjoys year-round sunshine with average temperatures of 80°F — and trade winds that keep everything comfortable.

🗺️ Things to Do

Explore the west end of Curaçao

⛰️ Mount Christoffel

Curaçao's highest peak at 372m, within Christoffel National Park. Hike through diverse flora to panoramic views. Climbing 6–10 AM; also accessible by jeep safari.

🌿 Hòfi Mango

Stunning 1707 nature park with 1,100+ ancient mango trees, a historic sugar mill, the "Stairway to Heaven" viewpoint, and Curaçao's first zipline.

🌊 Shete Boka National Park

Ten dramatic inlets where powerful waves crash against limestone cliffs. Natural blowholes, turtle nesting sites, and spectacular coastal hiking.

🏛️ Tula Museum (Landhuis Kenepa)

Historic plantation where Tula led the famous 1795 slave revolt. The preserved landhuis now serves as a museum with guided tours.

🍽️ Playa Forti

Dramatic clifftop restaurant with sweeping panoramic views of Westpunt Bay. A beloved local gathering spot at sunset.

🌆 Willemstad (45 min)

UNESCO World Heritage city with colorful Dutch colonial architecture, the floating Queen Emma Bridge, and vibrant Punda and Otrobanda districts.

Know Before You Go

About Curaçao

☀️ Climate

Year-round sunshine, avg. 80°F (27°C). Outside the hurricane belt. Trade winds provide natural cooling — comfortable even in summer.

🗣️ Language

Dutch and Papiamentu are official. English and Spanish are widely spoken. The island has rich African, European, and Latin American cultural roots.

💱 Currency

Caribbean Guilder (XCG). US dollars widely accepted. Credit cards accepted at most establishments. Car rental recommended for Westpunt.

✈️ Getting Here

Hato International Airport (CUR) with connections across the Americas and Europe. Westpunt is about 45 minutes from the airport.

🐠 Marine Life

65+ pristine dive sites. Healthy coral reefs, sea turtles, tropical fish, and exceptional underwater visibility. Shore diving from most beaches.

🚗 Transportation

A rental car is essential for exploring Westpunt and the island's remote beaches. Roads are well-maintained but can be narrow in rural areas.
